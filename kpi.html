How this model works

Online orders = website visits × online conversion rate. Store transactions = store visits × store purchase rate. Revenue = total transactions × average order value. ROAS = modeled revenue ÷ marketing spend. Cost per acquisition = marketing spend ÷ modeled transactions. Community engagements = outreach contacts × engagement rate; incremental store visits = engagements × referral rate. Month-by-month projections compound the selected baseline traffic growth rate. Regional purchaser and storefront visit figures are scenario-based expectations, not observed cross-channel tracking. One purchase is treated as one buyer for illustration, so repeat buyers can inflate counts. The assumed visit likelihood applies to each monthly purchaser cohort over the 12 months following purchase; cohort outcomes can extend beyond the displayed year. These are illustrative planning scenarios, not validated forecasts or causal attribution. In reality, not all revenue is attributable to marketing, customers may overlap across channels, and profitability requires cost-of-goods and operating expenses.
