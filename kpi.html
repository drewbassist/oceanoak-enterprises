How this model works

Online orders = website visits × online conversion rate. Store transactions = store visits × store purchase rate. Revenue = total transactions × average order value. ROAS = modeled revenue ÷ marketing spend. Cost per acquisition = marketing spend ÷ modeled transactions. Community engagements = outreach contacts × engagement rate; incremental store visits = engagements × referral rate. Month-by-month projections compound the selected baseline traffic growth rate. These are illustrative planning scenarios, not validated forecasts or causal attribution. In reality, not all revenue is attributable to marketing, customers may overlap across channels, and profitability requires cost-of-goods and operating expenses.
